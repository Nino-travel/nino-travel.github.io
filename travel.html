FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I book on your site?

You search here, then finish your booking directly with the travel company we send you to (for example Aviasales, Booking.com, Expedia, GetYourGuide, Viator, Discover Cars, or Airalo). Your ticket, payment, changes, and refunds are handled by that company, so you get their customer service and protections.

Does it cost more to book through Nino Travel?

No. Prices are set by the partner. When you book through our links we may earn a small commission from the partner, and you pay the same price you'd see going to them directly.

Why do flight results open on Aviasales?

Aviasales is a flight search engine that compares fares from many airlines and online agencies at once. You'll pick a fare there and complete the purchase with the airline or agency shown.

Is my payment information safe?

We never see or store your card details. Partner bookings are paid on the partner's own secure checkout. Our own guides and trip plans are paid through Stripe Checkout, a widely used payment processor, and we never receive your full card number.

My flight is delayed or cancelled. Can you help?

Yes, start with our Delay Help Center. It explains your options and rights in plain language and links to official sources. For changes to your existing booking, contact the airline or booking site you bought from; we can't change tickets for you.

What's included in a travel concierge plan?

Depending on the tier: a day-by-day itinerary, destination ideas that fit your budget, a shortlist of flights, stays, and activities with links, a total-cost estimate, and packing tips, delivered as a PDF by email. AI tools speed up the research, and a person reviews every plan. See service terms for timing, revisions, and what's not included.

Who runs Nino Travel?

Nino Travel is run by Atlanta creator YSSN Nino (@yssn_nino1). It's an independent site with no ownership ties to any airline or booking company.
